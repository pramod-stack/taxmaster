Frequently asked

Answers, before you ask.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. TAX MASTER supports salaried individuals, freelancers, professionals, SMEs and growing businesses across Bangalore.

How do we usually start?

The fastest way is to WhatsApp us or use the consultation form. We'll schedule a short call, understand your situation and share a clean list of next steps.

Do I need to visit your office?

Most work is handled remotely with secure document sharing. You're always welcome to visit our office in Amruthanagar if you prefer.

Do you handle income tax notices?

Yes — we regularly assist clients in understanding and responding to income tax notices. Share the notice on WhatsApp and we'll review it.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the type of work and its complexity. We'll always share a clear quote up front — no surprises.

Which areas of Bangalore do you serve?

Our office is in Sahakara Nagar / Amruthanagar. We serve clients across all of Bangalore, and remotely across India.
